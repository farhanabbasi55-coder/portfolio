Medicineonline.pk launch promo

A 25-second vertical video introducing my online pharmacy. It opens on the problem everyone recognises, an empty medicine strip, then shows both ways to order: browsing the website, or simply sending a photo of a prescription on WhatsApp.

As a pharmacist I know many customers would rather send a prescription than search a catalogue for brand names, so the WhatsApp route gets its own scene and its own button at the end.

Role: concept, script, storyboard, visual design Format: 9:16, 25 seconds, 6 scenes Tool: Canva
